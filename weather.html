Bangkok, Thailand — Severe flooding

Bangkok is experiencing major flooding after about 300 mm of rain fell in two days. Around 40 people have reportedly died, thousands have moved to shelters, and transportation has been badly disrupted. More heavy rain is forecast. The Guardian



Niño — Global weather concern

The World Meteorological Organization says the current El Niño is expected to strengthen significantly and continue into early 2027. It can change rainfall and temperature patterns and increase risks of extreme heat, floods and droughts in different parts of the world. World Meteorological Organization



Nepal — Glacial flood danger

Nepal is planning to drain four dangerous high-altitude glacial lakes after a recent glacier-related disaster killed more than 1,400 people and left thousands missing. The project is intended to reduce the risk of future floods. Reuters



California, USA — Unusual October heat

Southern California is experiencing an intense heat wave, with temperatures around 100°F (38°C) possible in parts of the region. The Washington Post



Greece — Heavy rain and strong winds

The island of Crete has experienced heavy rain, flooding and strong winds, with some ferry services cancelled and authorities advising people to avoid unnecessary travel.
