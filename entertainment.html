Bollywood: Drishyam 3 Gets Attention

Ajay Devgn's Drishyam 3 is receiving strong attention after its release in cinemas. The movie continues the popular mystery and thriller franchise. Audiences have been discussing the story and performances, making it one of the notable Bollywood entertainment releases of the week.

New Song Released for Udta Teer

A new song called Choozay from the upcoming movie Udta Teer has been released. The song features music by Emiway Bantai and is part of the film starring Ayushmann Khurrana and Sara Ali Khan. The movie is scheduled to arrive in cinemas on October 23, 2026.

Bollywood Music Project Celebrates 10 Years

Several well-known Indian singers are preparing to perform at the 10th anniversary edition of the Bollywood Music Project. Himesh Reshammiya, Hariharan and Anu Malik are among the artists announced for the event. The celebration brings together performers and fans of Bollywood music.

New Shows and Movies Arrive on Streaming Platforms

Several new movies and television shows are arriving on streaming platforms this week. The releases include drama, comedy, thriller, animation and documentary titles. Viewers can choose from both Indian and international content on different streaming services.

Pakistan: First Documentary Selected for Oscars

Hanging by a Wire has been selected as Pakistan's first documentary submission for the Oscars. The film tells the story of the dramatic cable car emergency that happened in Battagram in August 2023. The documentary has attracted attention because it presents a real event from Pakistan.
