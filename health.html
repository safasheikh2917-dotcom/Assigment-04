Pakistan: New Polio Case Reported

Pakistan has reported its fifth polio case of 2026 in southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Health authorities are continuing vaccination efforts to protect children from the disease. The government is also encouraging parents to make sure their children receive all recommended polio vaccination doses.

Bangladesh: Dengue Cases Increase

Bangladesh is facing a serious dengue outbreak, with health authorities warning that the situation could become worse during October. The country recorded a large increase in dengue-related deaths during September. Health experts are asking communities to remove stagnant water where mosquitoes can breed.

Sri Lanka: Dengue Remains a Health Concern

Sri Lanka has reported more than 100,000 dengue cases since the beginning of 2026. Health officials say the number of cases has declined from the peak seen during July, but dengue remains a public health concern. Colombo and Gampaha are among the areas reporting many cases.

Pakistan: Focus on Mental Health

Pakistan recently held a Global Mental Health Summit to discuss the country's mental healthcare needs. The summit highlighted the importance of giving mental health greater attention in public policy and healthcare services. Experts and officials discussed ways to improve mental health support across the country.

Air Quality and Public Health in Pakistan

Air quality continues to be an important public health issue in Pakistan. Recent environmental data shows that the country still faces significant challenges related to air pollution. Poor air quality can affect people's health and makes environmental protection an important part of public health.
