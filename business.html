Pakistan: Exports Increase

Pakistan's exports increased by 11 percent during the first quarter of the 2026-27 financial year. However, the country's trade deficit also became larger during the same period. The government and business community are focusing on increasing exports and improving the country's trade position.

Pakistan: Inflation Slows

Pakistan's consumer inflation slowed to 10.3 percent in September 2026, compared with 11.1 percent in August. The change was mainly linked to movements in prices of essential goods and services. Inflation remains an important issue for businesses and consumers across the country.

Pakistan: Private Sector to Lead Growth

Pakistan's finance minister has said that the private sector should play a major role in sustainable economic growth and investment. The government is also focusing on reforms, exports, digitalization and improving access to finance for small and medium-sized businesses.

Global Business: AI Investment Continues to Grow

Investment in artificial intelligence continues to have a major impact on global business. Companies are spending heavily on AI systems, data centres and related technologies. Growing demand for AI is also supporting activity in technology and manufacturing industries around the world.

Global Manufacturing Shows Growth

Global manufacturing activity improved in September 2026, reaching its highest level in several years. Factory production, new orders and employment all showed signs of improvement. However, rising costs and higher prices remain challenges for businesses in many countries.
