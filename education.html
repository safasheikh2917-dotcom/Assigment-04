Sindh: Drive to Bring Children Back to School

The Sindh government has started a province-wide enrolment drive to bring out-of-school children back to classrooms. A pilot project in Thatta enrolled 702 students in just nine days, including many girls and children who had previously left school. The government plans to expand this model across Sindh.

Karachi: Education Expo Brings Universities Together

An education expo was recently held in Karachi, bringing around 60 universities under one roof. Students were able to meet university representatives and learn about different degree programmes and career opportunities. The event helped students understand their options for higher education.

UNESCO: Education and Climate Change

UNESCO is working to place education at the centre of climate action. The organisation says students need knowledge and skills to understand climate change and respond to environmental challenges. Education is expected to play an important role in building climate-resilient communities.

Technology in Education

Technology is creating new opportunities for students and teachers around the world. UNESCO has highlighted how digital tools can support learning while teachers remain an important part of the classroom. Schools are increasingly exploring technology to make education more accessible and effective.

Education for Sustainable Development

UNESCO has introduced new guidance tools to help education systems include sustainable development in learning. The tools are designed to help students develop knowledge, skills and values for a more sustainable future. Several countries have already taken part in pilot programmes using these approaches.
